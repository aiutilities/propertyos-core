## 1. Purpose

This roadmap communicates the official public direction of PropertyOS.

It identifies:

- completed product work;
- the current Repository Foundation programme;
- the future open-source ecosystem;
- long-term platform direction;
- work that is not authorized during the current product freeze.

This roadmap is directional. It is not a contractual delivery commitment,
release guarantee or authorization to bypass project governance.

---

## 2. Product Positioning

> **PropertyOS — The Open Source AI-Native Operating System for Properties,
> Workspaces and Hospitality.**

PropertyOS provides shared infrastructure for residential properties, rental
operations, hostels, co-living, commercial properties, workspaces, hospitality,
facilities and communities.

---

## 3. Vision

> **Build infrastructure for the physical world.**

PropertyOS enables operators, developers and service providers to build
solutions without repeatedly rebuilding the same operational foundation.

## 5. Architectural Direction

PropertyOS is designed to remain:

- plugin-first;
- API-first;
- event-driven;
- AI-native;
- provider-agnostic;
- constitution-driven;
- open-source;
- modular;
- observable;
- self-hostable;
- ecosystem-oriented.

Future work should strengthen these characteristics rather than create
incompatible PropertyOS products.

## 7. Product-Freeze Declaration

Product feature development is frozen during Repository Foundation.

During the freeze:

- no new product features are authorized;
- product source must not change without explicit authorization;
- governance work must remain isolated;
- HEAD and changed-file scope must be validated;
- every milestone must close with a controlled commit;
- repository cleanliness must be restored;
- approved roadmap order must remain unchanged.

Unexpected repository state is an operational blocker, not permission to
introduce new work.

---

## 13. Ecosystem Direction

Plugins should provide specialization while Core remains stable.

Themes should support branding, portals, accessibility and role-specific
experiences without bypassing security.

AI Specialists should remain auditable, permission-aware, provider-agnostic,
human-accountable and safe by default.

Developer experience should make it easy to understand, run, extend, validate,
package and publish PropertyOS extensions through governed channels.

---

## 14. Community and Commercial Direction

The community should support contributors, operators, developers, publishers,
security researchers, implementation partners and users.

Commercial participation may include:

- enterprise support;
- implementation consulting;
- hosted PropertyOS;
- migrations and training;
- premium extensions;
- marketplace and integration services.

Commercial participation does not automatically grant governance, release,
security, maintainer, roadmap or trademark authority.
