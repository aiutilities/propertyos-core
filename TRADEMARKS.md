## 1. Purpose

PropertyOS is open-source software.

Its source-code license allows broad use, modification and distribution of covered software under the Mozilla Public License 2.0.

The source-code license does not grant unrestricted permission to use the PropertyOS name, logos, visual identity or other trademarks.

This policy protects users from confusion while allowing honest discussion, community participation, compatibility statements and responsible ecosystem development.

The PropertyOS name, word mark, logos, visual identity and associated project branding are owned and administered by:

This policy applies to marks including:

## 4. Uses That Are Generally Permitted

You may generally use the PropertyOS name without separate written permission when the use is truthful, descriptive and does not imply endorsement.

Examples include:

- referring to the PropertyOS project
- linking to the official repository or website
- stating that software works with PropertyOS
- stating that a plugin is compatible with PropertyOS
- writing articles, books, reviews or tutorials about PropertyOS
- discussing PropertyOS at events or in educational materials
- accurately identifying unmodified official PropertyOS software
- using the name in factual comparisons
- displaying the word PropertyOS in ordinary text to identify the project

Examples:

- “Compatible with PropertyOS”
- “Plugin for PropertyOS”
- “Training course about PropertyOS”
- “Independent consulting services for PropertyOS”

These uses must remain accurate and must not imply official status, certification, sponsorship or endorsement unless such status has been formally granted.

---

## 5. Uses Requiring Written Permission

Written permission from Cogzidel Technologies Pvt. Ltd. is required before:

- using a PropertyOS logo as the primary identity of another product
- incorporating PropertyOS into a company or organization name
- incorporating PropertyOS into a commercial product name
- registering a domain name primarily based on the PropertyOS mark
- registering a social-media account that appears official
- manufacturing merchandise using official PropertyOS branding
- representing a product as an official PropertyOS edition
- claiming certification, partnership or endorsement
- modifying an official logo
- using official branding in advertising in a way that suggests affiliation

Permission may be subject to brand, quality and attribution requirements.

---

## 6. Forks and Modified Distributions

The MPL-2.0 permits modification and redistribution of covered source code when its requirements are followed.

A materially modified distribution must not present itself as the official PropertyOS release.

Unless written permission is granted, modified distributions must:

- use a distinct product name
- use distinct logos and branding
- clearly state that they are independently maintained
- avoid implying official endorsement
- preserve required copyright and license notices
- identify the original PropertyOS source accurately

A fork may truthfully state:

> Based on PropertyOS

It may not state or imply:

> Official PropertyOS

unless authorized by Cogzidel Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

---

## 7. Plugins, Themes and Integrations

Independent plugins, themes and integrations may state compatibility with PropertyOS.

Recommended naming patterns include:

- “Example Plugin for PropertyOS”
- “Example Theme for PropertyOS”
- “Example Connector compatible with PropertyOS”

Avoid naming patterns that imply ownership or official status, such as:

- “PropertyOS Official Example”
- “PropertyOS Enterprise”
- “PropertyOS Cloud”
- “PropertyOS Certified”

unless authorization has been granted.

The product’s own brand should be more prominent than the PropertyOS compatibility reference.

---

## 8. Community Groups and Events

Community groups, meetups and events may use the PropertyOS name descriptively if they:

- clearly identify themselves as community-led
- do not imply official sponsorship
- do not misuse official logos
- do not engage in misleading, unlawful or harmful conduct
- follow the PropertyOS Code of Conduct when representing the community

Recommended descriptions include:

- “PropertyOS Chennai Community”
- “Independent PropertyOS User Group”
- “Community event for PropertyOS users”

Official status requires written authorization.

---

## 9. Domains and Social Accounts

Do not register domains, application-store listings or social-media accounts that could reasonably be mistaken for official PropertyOS channels.

Unauthorized examples may include names such as:

- official-propertyos
- propertyos-foundation
- propertyos-cloud
- propertyos-support
- propertyos-security

Descriptive or fan/community uses may be acceptable when their independent status is immediately clear.

---

## 10. No Misleading Use

PropertyOS marks must not be used:

- deceptively
- fraudulently
- unlawfully
- in connection with malware
- in connection with unauthorized surveillance
- in connection with security misrepresentation
- to impersonate the project or its maintainers
- to suggest certification that has not been granted
- in ways that damage user trust through confusion

---

## 11. Quality and Reputation

Permission to use PropertyOS trademarks may be withdrawn when branding is used in connection with:

- materially insecure distributions
- intentionally misleading services
- unsupported claims of compatibility
- fraudulent products
- violations of applicable law
- conduct that creates significant confusion about the official project

Trademark enforcement should be proportionate, transparent and focused on preventing confusion or harm.

## 13. Source Code and Trademark Rights Are Separate

The right to use, modify or distribute PropertyOS source code under MPL-2.0 does not automatically include the right to use PropertyOS trademarks.

Likewise, permission to use a PropertyOS trademark does not change the source-code license.

Both sets of requirements must be followed independently.

---

## 14. Permission Requests

Requests for trademark permission should include:

- requester name
- organization
- contact information
- intended use
- product or service description
- proposed wording
- proposed visual presentation
- countries or markets involved
- expected duration of use

An official trademark contact channel will be published in the project governance documentation.

Until then, requests may be submitted through a private contact channel operated by Cogzidel Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

Do not include confidential information in a public GitHub issue.

---

## 15. Enforcement Principles

Cogzidel Technologies Pvt. Ltd. intends to administer PropertyOS trademarks according to these principles:

- protect users from confusion
- preserve the integrity of the official project
- permit honest descriptive use
- support a healthy plugin and service ecosystem
- act proportionately
- distinguish good-faith mistakes from intentional abuse
- provide reasonable opportunities to correct non-malicious misuse
