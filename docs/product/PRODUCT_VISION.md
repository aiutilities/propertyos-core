# Vision

PropertyOS is an open-source operating system for managing physical spaces.

Rather than being a traditional Property Management System (PMS), PropertyOS provides a modular, extensible, event-driven platform capable of operating apartments, gated communities, commercial buildings, warehouses, campuses, hospitals, hotels, factories, hostels, and future smart infrastructure.

The goal is to become the "Linux of Property Management"—an open platform upon which organizations can build, customize, and extend their operations.

---

# Mission

Build the world's most extensible open-source platform for managing physical assets, people, facilities, inventory, operations, and services.

Every organization should be able to deploy PropertyOS, customize it through plugins, integrate it with existing systems, and automate workflows without vendor lock-in.

# Success Criteria

PropertyOS succeeds when:

- Organizations can deploy it in under 30 minutes.
- Developers can create plugins without modifying the core.
- Businesses can operate entirely on PropertyOS.
- The community contributes modules independently.
- AI agents can extend functionality safely.
